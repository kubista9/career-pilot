# Cover letter rules (example)

Copy this file to the path in `sources.letter_rules` and adapt it. The agent follows it for every letter.
`career-pilot letter-check` enforces the mechanical parts (length, paragraphs, dashes, phrases) from
the `letters` section of the profile.

## Shape

- Write in English, even when the listing is in another language.
- At most four paragraphs and 2,000 characters including spaces.
- Greeting: "Dear <name>," when the listing names the hiring manager, otherwise "Dear <Company> hiring team,".

## Content

1. Why this company and this role, with one specific thing taken from the listing.
2. Two or three experiences from the chosen CV that fit the role. One personal detail only if it truly fits.
3. What I bring, when I am available and for how many hours, and a short closing line.

## Style

- Hyphens only, no em or en dashes.
- Plain words. No clichés, no claims I cannot prove, no invented experience.
- Languages: say my level exactly as written in the facts file.
- Read the finished letter once as the hiring manager would. Cut anything that could be in any letter.
